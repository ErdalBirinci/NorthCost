💡 How is it calculated?

Figures reflect November 2026 price levels — 2026 transit fares, the EU fuel bulletin, ECB November 2026 rates and national Q3 2026 rent statistics. Heating peaks in the far north, rents in the capitals. Salaries are average net estimates.

  • 🏠 Rent: centre + suburbs weighted
  • 🛒 Groceries: 25-item basket
  • 🚌 Transport + ⚡ utilities included

📚 Data sources

All figures are November 2026 estimates anchored to these publications:

  • Statistics Finland — rents Q3 2026
  • HSL — 2026 fares
  • Nysse Tampere — 2026 fares
  • Föli Turku — tickets
  • EU Weekly Oil Bulletin — petrol
  • ECB — euro reference rate, 13 Nov 2026
  • S-kaupat — grocery listings
  • Taksi Helsinki — tariff
  • SCB — Swedish rents & earnings
  • Statistics Norway — prices & rental market
  • Statistics Denmark — rents & housing
  • SL Stockholm — 2026 fares
  • Ruter Oslo — 2026 fares
  • DOT Copenhagen — 2026 fares
  • HikersBay Helsinki — Nov 2026 snapshot
  • Statistics Finland — CPI & earnings

City rents use Nov 2026 crowd surveys; smaller areas are scaled from official €/m² statistics. Photos: Wikimedia Commons.
